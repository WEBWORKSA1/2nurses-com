Every nurse deserves a second nurse.

Nursing is a team sport. The best advice most nurses ever got came from another nurse at 3 a.m. — on how to hang a drip faster, survive a first code, or negotiate a contract. 2Nurses exists to make that second nurse available to everyone.

🎯

Mission

Help every nurse and future nurse make better career, education and money decisions — for free.

🧪

Editorial standards

Guides are written or reviewed by licensed nurses, cite public sources, and are updated regularly. Sponsored content is always labeled.

💡

How we stay free

Advertising (including Google AdSense), sponsorships, partner referrals and reader donations. Partners never buy editorial opinions.

What we build

  • Matching: school, travel/job and employer funnels that connect people with accredited programs and vetted partners.
  • Tools: the salary explorer, travel pay and shift calculators, dosage practice and the specialty quiz.
  • Learning: free NCLEX-style practice, guides and videos.
  • Community: Mentor Match, Nurse of the Month, stories, contests and scholarships.

Corrections

Found an error? Tell us through the contact form (choose “Correction”). We review every report and note material updates on the page.
