Matching FAQ

How much experience do I need for travel nursing?

Most agencies require at least 1–2 years of recent experience in your specialty. Some local and per diem roles accept newer nurses.

Do you only work with U.S. programs?

We focus on U.S. and Canadian programs and employers, and we also accept requests from internationally educated nurses exploring NCLEX and U.S. placement.

How is 2Nurses paid?

Partner schools, agencies and employers pay us to reach qualified candidates. That keeps the service free for you, and it never changes the pay or tuition you are offered.

Can I delete my information?

Yes. Use our contact form and choose “Privacy request”.
