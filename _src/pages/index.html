Frequently asked

Is 2Nurses really free for nurses and students?

Yes. Matching, tools, NCLEX practice and community features are free. We earn from advertising, sponsorships and from partner schools and employers who pay to reach qualified candidates.

Who will contact me after I get matched?

Only the vetted partners you consent to (up to 3 schools or up to 4 staffing partners) and the 2Nurses coordinator. You can opt out at any time.

Are salary figures exact?

No. They are estimates compiled from public wage data and adjusted for recency. Always confirm with employers and the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics.

Is 2Nurses connected to other “two nurses” brands?

No. 2Nurses.com is an independent publication and is not affiliated with any other business, podcast, or channel using a similar name. See our trademark notice.
