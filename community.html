Recognition

Nominate a Nurse of the Month

Patients, families, colleagues and managers can nominate any nurse, CNA, LPN, NP or nursing student. Each month’s honoree receives a published feature, a digital certificate and a sponsored thank-you gift. Teams can be nominated too.

🏅 Categories

Bedside Hero · Rising Star (new grad or student) · Team Award · Nurse Leader · Educator · Lifetime of Care

🎁 Honoree receives

A feature story, a certificate, a social share card, a gift from our prize sponsor, and an invitation to the annual honoree showcase.
