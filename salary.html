Understanding nurse pay

Where you work matters about as much as what you do. West Coast and Northeast states lead on raw pay. After adjusting for housing and everyday costs, the ranking changes: several mid-cost states pull ahead because each dollar goes further.

Base pay vs. total compensation

Your hourly base is only part of the picture. Shift differentials (often $2–$8/hr for nights), weekend premiums, overtime at 1.5×, sign-on bonuses, and tuition reimbursement can add 10–25% to annual earnings. Travel contracts add tax-free housing and meal stipends when you keep a qualifying tax home.

Credentials that change the curve

Moving from LPN to RN, or from RN to NP or CRNA, is the biggest pay lever in nursing. Use our school match to compare accredited bridge programs, including online RN-to-BSN and NP tracks.
